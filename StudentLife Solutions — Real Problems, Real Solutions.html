Latest Student Opportunities

Verified official portals — apply directly on the source website.

National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

Pan-India • Central & State schemes

The official central portal for all Government of India scholarships — for students from SC/ST/OBC/Minority/EBC categories, post-matric, pre-matric, merit-cum-means and more.

Verify details on the official portal

MahaDBT — Maharashtra Direct Benefit Transfer

Maharashtra • State schemes

Official portal of the Government of Maharashtra for state-administered scholarships for backward classes, minorities, economically backward students and SC/ST/OBC/VJNT/SBC.

Verify details on the official portal

AICTE — Pragati & Saksham Schemes

Pan-India • Technical education

All India Council for Technical Education schemes — Pragati (for girls) and Saksham (for differently-abled students) for technical education.

Verify details on the official portal

National Fellowship for Higher Education

Pan-India • Higher education

University Grants Commission fellowships for SC/ST and minority students pursuing MPhil/PhD. Verify current notifications on the UGC portal.

Verify details on the official portal
